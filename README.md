## Methodology
My team and I examined 3 cities (Baltimore, Pittsburgh, and New Orleans), using 4 estimators: Pre/post growth means, 
PCI & PCE vs Win Rate Regression, Stock Market ttest, and Sectoral DiD to assess whether winning a championship league, particularly NFL, 
boosts a city's economy.


## Results
Championships look bigger in the news than in the data. Our data and analyses show that there was little to no effect on the city's economy after winning a championship league, at least not a sustainable one. 

### Aggregate:
No statistically significant lift in real GDP or per-capita income
### Short-run:
No discernible jump in L&H employment around the cinch months
### Sectoral:
A real but small Arts/Entertainment/Recreational lift exists, but it is too tiny to move the macro needle
